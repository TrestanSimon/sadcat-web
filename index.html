Supra-Arcade Downflow Catalog

The following is a catalog of solar flares with supra-arcade downflows (SADs) imaged during solar cycle 24 by the Atmospheric Imaging Assembly (AIA) aboard the Solar Dynamics Observatory (SDO). A full description of the catalog can be found in the accompanying publication. The symbol definitions, acknowledgements, and information about citing the SADCat are provided below the catalog.

Acknowledgements

Flare data came from the Hinode Flare Catalogue at https://hinode.isee.nagoya-u.ac.jp/flare_catalogue/, which is maintained by ISAS/JAXA and Institute for Space-Earth Environmental Research (ISEE), Nagoya University.

CME data came from the SOHO LASCO CME Catalog at https://cdaw.gsfc.nasa.gov/CME_list/index.html, which is generated and maintained at the CDAW Data Center by NASA and The Catholic University of America in cooperation with the Naval Research Laboratory. SOHO is a project of international cooperation between ESA and NASA.

The SDO data used is courtesy of NASA/SDO and the AIA, EVE, and HMI science teams.
